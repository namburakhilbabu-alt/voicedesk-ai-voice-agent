# Smile Dental Clinic: information for the receptionist

## Timings
Open Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM. Lunch break 1 PM to 2 PM. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
Appointments are 30 minutes long. Walk-ins are accepted only for emergencies.

## Location and parking
2nd floor, Lotus Plaza, 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Nearest metro: Indiranagar (5 minute walk).
Free parking in the building basement for two-wheelers and cars.

## Prices
Consultation and check-up: Rs 500 (free if you book a treatment the same day).
Teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing): Rs 1,500.
Tooth-coloured filling: from Rs 1,200 per tooth.
Root canal treatment: Rs 6,000 to Rs 9,000 depending on the tooth, crown extra.
Dental crown: from Rs 8,000 (metal-free ceramic from Rs 12,000).
Teeth whitening: Rs 12,000 for an in-clinic session.
Braces: metal braces from Rs 35,000; clear aligners from Rs 90,000. Free orthodontic consultation.
Dental implant: from Rs 30,000 per tooth.
Tooth extraction: Rs 1,000 simple, Rs 3,500 to Rs 6,000 for wisdom teeth.

## Payment and insurance
We accept cash, UPI, and all major cards. No-cost EMI is available for treatments above Rs 20,000.
We are not directly empanelled with insurance companies, but we provide all bills and reports for reimbursement claims.

## Doctors
Dr. Kavya Menon (BDS, MDS Orthodontics): braces, aligners, smile design.
Dr. Arjun Shetty (BDS, MDS Endodontics): root canals, fillings, pain treatment.
Both doctors are available on all working days.

## Emergencies
For severe tooth pain, swelling, bleeding, or a broken tooth, come in the same day; emergency patients are seen between appointments.
After hours, go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Cancellations
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before the appointment. There is no cancellation fee.

## Children
We treat children above 4 years old. Kids' check-ups are Rs 400.

## First visit
Please arrive 10 minutes early and bring any previous dental X-rays or reports. Bring a list of current medicines.
